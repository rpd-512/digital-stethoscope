⚙ SIGNAL PARAMETERS
Bandpass — Upper Cutoff Frequency 500 Hz
Controls the upper frequency limit of the bandpass filter. Heart sounds are typically 20–500 Hz. Increase to capture higher harmonics.
Bandpass — Lower Cutoff Frequency 30 Hz
Lower bound of bandpass filter. Reducing this can capture very low frequency components but may increase baseline noise.
Amplitude Limit (AMP_LIMIT) 1.5
Clips envelope amplitude to reduce motion artifact and noise spikes. Reduce for quieter signals; increase for stronger cardiac sounds.
